AFTERCARE

Extension Care Guide

1

Gentle Washing

Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Wash 2-3 times per week, avoiding the bonds.

2

Brush Daily

Use an extension-safe brush. Start from the ends and work your way up gently.

3

Heat Protection

Always use heat protectant spray before styling. Keep heat tools below 350°F.

4

Regular Maintenance

Schedule move-up appointments every 6-8 weeks to keep extensions looking fresh.
